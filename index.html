About AC Classics Montgrí

We are a group of enthusiasts from the Montgrí region who share a deep passion for classic automobiles. Our club brings together owners and lovers of timeless cars for drives, meetings, and unforgettable moments on the beautiful Costa Brava.

Featured Classics

AC Cobra

The legendary British-American sports car. A symbol of raw power and elegance.

Jaguar E-Type

Often called the most beautiful car ever made. Several examples grace our club.

Air-Cooled Porsche 911

Classic 911s from the golden era of Porsche engineering.
