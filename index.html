About Us

AC Classics Montgrí is a friendly club of enthusiasts dedicated to the preservation, enjoyment, and celebration of classic automobiles in the beautiful region of Montgrí, Girona.

We organize regular meetings, scenic drives along the Costa Brava, technical talks, and participation in national and international classic car events.

Featured Classics

AC Cobra

Iconic British-American sports car. The legend lives on in our club.

Jaguar E-Type

The most beautiful car ever made – several examples in the club.

Porsche 911

Air-cooled classics from the golden era of Porsche.
